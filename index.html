What we do

Full-service support for the systems that keep your home running.

From major appliances to safer dryer vents and everyday electrical or plumbing fixes — QSS handles the work Orange County homes rely on.

  • All Major Appliance Repair

    Fast, careful diagnostics and repair for washers, dryers, refrigerators, ovens, dishwashers, and more.

  • All Major Appliance Installations

    Clean, code-conscious installs so new appliances fit, connect, and perform the way they should.

  • All Major Appliance Maintenance

    Preventive care that extends equipment life and helps you avoid surprise breakdowns.

  • Dryer Fire Prevention

    Lint buildup is a leading fire risk. We help keep dryers safer for your family and home.

  • Dryer Duct Cleaning

    Thorough duct clearing to restore airflow, improve efficiency, and reduce overheating risk.

  • Whole Home Duct Cleanings

    Whole-home duct service to support cleaner air and better HVAC performance.

  • Light Plumbing

    Helpful plumbing support for everyday household needs without the wait for a full remodel crew.

  • Light Electrical

    Outlets, fixtures, panels checks, and other light electrical work handled with care and clarity.
